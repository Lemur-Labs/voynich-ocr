## Interpretations made in code (G2-3)

- **Line-to-locus pairing (VO-034, supersedes VO-013 for G2):** by count when counts match and the labelled lines
  pass training's `lines.screen`, otherwise by text alignment to ZL tokens. On test pages: 21 pages by count, 15
  by text. Text pairing reads ZL, so on those pages ZL helps choose which lines are scored.
- **Crops (VO-035):** the line polygon between the midpoints of CTC frame gaps, after kraken's own cuts gave
  slivers on 14.6% of non-test crops (now 1.1%). This was changed after a validation dry run whose outcome was kill,
  and the trigger was the sliver rate. Rows the baseline fit can't map keep kraken's cut; their count wasn't
  measured.
- **k = 220 (VO-036):** twice the distinct non-space tokens in the recognizer's training and early-stopping labels.
  The codec's 116 entries would have given 232.
- **Entropy pool (VO-038):** gated test instances only.
- **Control-arm strata:** under the prereg's column count, control crops can land on gap columns with no ZL
  reading. They form a `zl = ""` stratum the primary arm lacks.
