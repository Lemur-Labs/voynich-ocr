## Writeup additions (VO-041 to VO-043)

**Statement:** kill by the pre-agreed rule. At this test's power, the images do not explain the disagreements. That
is "not shown", not "shown absent".

**Effect and power.** The gated gap is 0.057, 95% CI [−0.017, 0.128]. With the standard error taken from the
distance to the lower bound (0.038, normal approximation, null fixed; VO-046), the rule detects a true gap of
**0.164** with 80% power. At the observed 0.057, the chance of passing is 2.4% (`g2.gate_power`).

**Protocol deviation (VO-041).** ZL helped choose which test lines were scored on all 36 pages: by text alignment
on 15 and by the word-length check on 21. Pairing used to select the population on the label being tested
could bias the result either way. The kill stands. Within a line, glyphs are placed in columns by the all-source
consensus, ZL included, as the prereg defines instances; no split removes that.
