## Claims allowed (prereg)

- **Allowed:** "recognizer confidence predicts where transliterations disagree, beyond glyph type and page".
- **"Calibrated":** permitted, since ECE 0.012 ≤ 0.05. That ECE is measured on the leave-{ZL, IT}-out map's 10,903
  glyphs, against agreement with that map's column majority, not against a ground truth.
- **"Independent of ZL":** permitted, since the leave-{ZL, IT}-out lower bound is 0.634 > 0.5. Three ways ZL still
  enters:
  - the model was trained on ZL labels, and T was fitted on the validation pages' ZL labels;
  - ZL helped choose the scored lines (VO-047);
  - the leave-out maps drop ZL's readings, not ZL's line pairing;
  - the leave-{ZL, IT}-out map scores a smaller population (a column is tier 3 only when GC, FG and CD all
    contribute) and has no coverage check or baseline bar. The prereg allows this, but the claim rests on it.
